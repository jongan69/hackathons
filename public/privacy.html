Privacy

Last updated August 9, 2026

Hackathons Board has no accounts, first-party analytics, or advertising trackers. The app does not set cookies or store personal information in your browser.

What the site receives

The site loads a public event feed from its own domain. Its hosting provider may process standard request information such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamp to deliver and protect the service.

External links

Event listings link to organizers, and support or sponsorship links may open GitHub or a configured payment provider. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Hackathons Board does not receive payment-card details.

Questions

Use the support page to report a privacy concern through the public GitHub repository. Do not include secrets or sensitive personal information in a public issue.
